Quick reference

Every gesture and default shortcut on one page. Print it and keep it next to your keyboard for the first week. The guide explains each one.

Snap

Do thisTo
⇧ dragShow the Zones; release over one to Snap the window there.
⇧⌃ dragSpan: every Zone you cross is added; the window fills them all.
Release ⇧, or escCancel the Snap during a drag.
⌘ on the green buttonOpen the Zone picker; click a Zone to Snap.
⌃-click in the pickerAdd Zones to a span; the next plain click Snaps across them.
Drag a shared edgeResize two Snapped neighbours together.

Default shortcuts

ShortcutAction
⌃⌥⇧1 to 9Snap to that Zone.
⌃⌥⇧[Previous Zone.
⌃⌥⇧]Next Zone.
⌃⌥⇧`Next Layout on this display.

Change them in Settings > Hotkeys. Zones are numbered top to bottom, then left to right.

Layout editor

Do thisTo
Click a ZoneSplit it across its longer side.
⇧-clickSplit the other way.
Drag a dividerResize, in 5% steps.
⌘-click, then ⌘JSelect Zones, then merge them.
Right-click a ZoneMake Rail, or Remove Rail.
⌘Z / ⇧⌘ZUndo / redo.
Done / escSave / discard.

The Rail

WhenWhat happens
Busy for 20 sThe session’s window moves to the Rail; its neighbour grows.
WaitingThe window goes back to its Zone, without taking focus.
Click itIt goes back at once.
Drag or resize itZonal lets go of it until you Snap it again.
Quit, switch LayoutEvery window in the Rail goes back.

Words Zonal uses

Zone
A rectangular region of a display that a window can be Snapped into.
Layout
A named arrangement of Zones covering one display.
Snap
To move and resize a window so it exactly fills a Zone.
Zone picker
A miniature of a display’s Layout, opened from a window’s green button, that Snaps the window to the Zones clicked.
Shared edge
The line where the Zones of two Snapped windows meet; resizing one window along it resizes the other.
Rail
A Zone of a Layout where windows whose work needs no attention wait at a smaller size, until they need you again and go back to the Zone they came from.
Busy
A window whose agent is working and needs nothing from you.
Waiting
A window whose agent has stopped and needs you: it finished, asks permission, or asks a question.
Trial
14 days on one Mac with every feature unlocked, before a License is needed.
License
A purchase that unlocks Zonal for one major version on up to three Macs.
Activation
One Mac’s claim on a License.
