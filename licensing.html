The free Trial

Zonal includes a 14-day Trial with every feature turned on. It starts the first time you open Zonal on a Mac. You do not need an account or a payment card.

While the Trial runs, the menu bar menu shows how many days are left, for example Trial: 12 days left.

When the Trial ends:

  • Snapping and hotkeys stop working.
  • The Layouts window and the Layout editor still open, and your Layouts are kept.
  • The menu shows Trial ended and offers Buy Zonal….

Activating a License turns everything back on. You do not need to reinstall or set anything up again.

Buying a License

Zonal is sold through Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy handles payment, VAT or sales tax, and your receipt. Your License key arrives by email from Lemon Squeezy after you pay.

Zonal is not on sale yet. When it is, you can buy from this page or from Buy Zonal… in the menu bar menu or in Settings.

Activating your License

  1. Open Settings. Choose Settings from the Zonal menu bar menu, then open the License tab.
  2. Enter your key. Paste the License key from your email into the License key field.
  3. Click Activate. The menu then shows Licensed, and the License tab shows Deactivate this Mac in place of the key field.

Activation needs an internet connection once, on each Mac. Afterward, Zonal works offline.

About every 30 days, Zonal checks your License with Lemon Squeezy in the background. If it cannot reach the server, Zonal keeps working for a 30-day grace period, and the menu shows License needs checking before a date. When Zonal can connect again, it checks the License and the message goes away. If the grace period ends first, Snapping and hotkeys stop until the next successful check.

Up to three Macs

One License is for your own use on up to three of your Macs. Each Mac you activate is one Activation.

If the License is already active on three Macs, a fourth Mac shows this message and stays as it was:

This license key is already active on 3 Macs. Deactivate one of them first, then try again.

To move a License to a new Mac:

  1. Deactivate the old Mac. On the old Mac, open Settings, then the License tab, and click Deactivate this Mac. This needs an internet connection.
  2. Activate the new Mac. On the new Mac, enter your License key and click Activate.

After you deactivate a Mac, it follows its Trial clock. If that Mac has already used its 14 days, Snapping and hotkeys stay off there.

Updates and major versions

Every 1.x update is included with your License. Zonal checks for updates daily and asks before it installs one, unless you turn on automatic installs in Settings > Updates.

A future 2.0 would be a separate purchase. Owners of a 1.x License would be offered a discounted upgrade. Zonal 1.x keeps working either way, and a key only works with its own major version: Zonal 1.x needs a 1.x key.

Refunds

Lemon Squeezy processes refunds. See the Terms for refund conditions.

A refunded License stops working. Zonal notices at its next check, and then Snapping and hotkeys stop on your Macs.
