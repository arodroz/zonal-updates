Your Layout, one gesture away

A Layout is a set of Zones for one display. Snap a window and it fills a Zone exactly.

  • Drag, then Snap

    Hold ⇧ while you drag a window and your Zones appear. Let go over one to Snap. Add ⌃ to sweep across several Zones and fill them all.

  • Hotkeys for the rest

    Hold ⌃⌥⇧ and press a number to Snap the focused window to that Zone. [ and ] step to the previous or next Zone, and ` switches Layout. Every shortcut can be changed.

  • A Layout editor at real size

    Split a Zone with a click, drag dividers to resize, and merge neighbors. Each display gets its own Layout, and you edit it at the size you will use it.

  • Plays well with macOS

    Works across Spaces, with Stage Manager, and alongside the tiling built into macOS. Windows that refuse a Zone's size are centered in it instead of falling off the screen.

One permission

Zonal asks for Accessibility and nothing else: no Input Monitoring, no Screen Recording. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

How it works

  1. Grant AccessibilityZonal needs it to move other apps' windows. It is the only permission.
  2. Pick or edit a LayoutStart from a ready-made Layout, or build your own for each display.
  3. Shift-drag a windowRelease it over a Zone. It Snaps into place.
