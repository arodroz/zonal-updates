Your Layout, one gesture away

A Layout is a set of Zones for one display. Snap a window and it fills a Zone exactly.

  • Drag, then Snap

    Hold ⇧ while you drag a window and your Zones appear. Let go over one to Snap. Add ⌃ to sweep across several Zones and fill them all.

  • Hotkeys for the rest

    Hold ⌃⌥⇧ and press a number to Snap the focused window to that Zone. [ and ] step to the previous or next Zone, and ` switches Layout. Every shortcut can be changed.

  • A Layout editor at real size

    Split a Zone with a click, drag dividers to resize, and merge neighbors. Each display gets its own Layout, and you edit it at the size you will use it.

  • Snap from the green button

    Hold ⌘ on a window's green button and a miniature of your Layout opens. Click a Zone and the window Snaps there, no drag needed. Made for trackpads.

  • Resize side by side

    Drag the edge between two Snapped windows and both follow, like panes in one app. No gaps, no overlap, and your Layout stays as it was.

  • Plays well with macOS

    Works across Spaces, with Stage Manager, and alongside the tiling built into macOS. Windows that refuse a Zone's size are centered in it instead of falling off the screen.

One permission

Zonal asks for Accessibility: no Input Monitoring, no Screen Recording. The optional Rail also asks to control your terminal app, only to find a session's window. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

Running coding agents? Meet the Rail

Mark one Zone as the Rail. When a Claude Code session has been working for 20 seconds, its terminal slides into the Rail, still readable, and its neighbour takes the space. The moment the session needs you, the window is back in its Zone, without stealing focus. One click in Settings connects Claude Code. How the Rail works

How it works

  1. Grant AccessibilityZonal needs it to move other apps' windows. It is the one permission Snapping needs.
  2. Pick or edit a LayoutStart from a ready-made Layout, or build your own for each display.
  3. Shift-drag a windowRelease it over a Zone. It Snaps into place.
